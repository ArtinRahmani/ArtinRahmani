Artin

A Mind Full of Questions. A Journey Built Through Discipline.

I am a high school student in the experimental sciences track, interested in science, technology, and understanding the world beyond what is written in textbooks.

Since childhood, people around me often described me as an intelligent person. For a long time, I never took that seriously. From my perspective, intelligence meant little without something meaningful to show for it.

Looking back, I believe both perspectives had some truth.

I may have had the potential, but I had not developed the discipline and consistency needed to turn that potential into tangible achievements.

Today, I see things differently.

I am not here to claim that I have already accomplished great things. I am here because I have decided to change the way I approach learning, work, and personal growth.

My Interests

- Science and Critical Thinking
- Medicine and Biology
- Technology and Programming
- Problem Solving
- Scientific Research

What I Am Working On

I am beginning to explore the intersection of medicine, biology, and technology while developing my programming and analytical skills.

My focus is on learning through practice, building meaningful projects, and documenting my progress honestly.

My Philosophy

Intelligence may create potential, but discipline gives it direction.

I don't want to be known only for what I could become. I want my work to show what I actually built.

The Journey Ahead

This profile is a record of that journey — its projects, lessons, mistakes, and progress.

Not just potential. Proof through action.
